# Required Implementation — What We Must Build Ourselves

Derived from `experiment_reconstruction_plan.md` and the gap analysis in `paper_vs_repository.md`.

**Scope rule.** Everything below is written by us from the paper. Where the author repository supplies
a fact the paper omits (anchors, decode formulas, dequant scale, I/O contract, protocol constants),
we take **the fact** and write **our own code**. We do not vendor or execute upstream code.

**Estimate of split:** ~85 % of the pipeline must be written from the paper; ~15 % exists upstream as
a usable reference. The two artifacts the paper explicitly delegates to the repository — the folding
configuration and the FIFO depths — are **absent there**, so they must be re-derived (plan §T1.6/T1.7).

### 2.2 Model and training — T1.2, T1.3

| File | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `models/quant_common.py` | **[OURS+PAPER]** | Brevitas `QuantConv2d`, `QuantReLU`, `QuantHardTanh` wrappers. | `models/yolov3-tiny-pruned-4w4a.yaml` | **[OURS+PAPER]** | Table 1 channel widths: 8, 8, 16, 32, 56, 104, 208, 56, 104, 18. |

| `train.py`, `val.py` | **[OURS+PAPER]** | Table 3: 300 epochs, batch 64, 416², SGD, patience 30. A single mAP implementation shared by T1.2/T1.3 so the comparison is internally valid. |
| `utils/loss.py` | **[OURS]** | Must match the `detect.py` parameterisation, not stock YOLOv5's. Non-trivial: the quadratic `wh` and affine `obj` terms change the gradients. |

## 5. What we are deliberately doing differently from the paper

These are improvements, not deviations to hide. Each is recorded here and will be flagged in results.

| # | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **I1** | Add **T1.12 functional equivalence** (PyTorch ↔ QONNX ↔ cppsim ↔ rtlsim) | §4.2.2 asserts correctness with no quantitative evidence, and the repo's `validate.py` is unmodified MNIST/CIFAR-10 boilerplate. Nothing shows the FPGA computes the trained function. |
| **I2** | Report **mean ± σ over ≥10 repetitions** for all throughput numbers | Table 9 reports bare single values. |
| **I3** | **Measure** Coral power on the same bench as the FPGA | The paper's Coral power is a datasheet estimate, and 1.2 W is obtained by halving 2.4 W. Table 15 is otherwise asymmetric. |
| **I4** | **Measure** PL-only power by differencing loaded/unloaded bitstream | §4.3.2 derives the 0.22–0.65 W PL figure arithmetically. |
| **I5** | State **batch regime per row** in the Table 16 equivalent | The paper's Table 16 uses the batch-100 latency (4.79 ms) while Table 15 uses batch-1 (6.07 ms); mixing regimes across comparison tables flatters the result. |
| **I6** | Quantify **T2.5 field performance** with hand-labelled frames | §4.5's claim ("almost all cars … detected") is purely qualitative, and the repo's `conf=0.10/iou=0.10` thresholds are permissive enough to inflate apparent recall. |
| **I7** | Use a **>30 FPS source** in T2.4 | The paper's 30 FPS may be camera-limited rather than pipeline-limited; it does not separate the two. |
| **I8** | Report the **Edge TPU op-mapping fraction** in T1.13 | Ops falling back to CPU would materially change T3.1, and the paper never reports this. |
| **I9** | Hard **assertions** in the FINN front-end (1 partition, 0 float ops, 2 Transpose + 1 Mul) | §3.4.4 makes these correctness requirements; they should fail loudly, not silently. |
| **I10** | Verify our architecture against **Table 6 weight counts** before any synthesis | Weight counts are folding-independent, so an exact match is a cheap, strong proof the T1.3 model matches the paper. A mismatch means our architecture is wrong. |

## 7. First recommended action after approval

A **feasibility spike**, not T1.1: install FINN 0.10 + Vivado 2022.1 on `/mnt/data10tb` and push one
of FINN's own example networks (TFC or CNV) end-to-end to a 7z020 bitstream plus an rtlsim run.

This validates, cheaply and early:
- the **15 GB RAM** ceiling against Verilator and Vitis HLS (the plan's top technical risk),
- that **FINN 0.10 pairs with Vivado 2022.1**,
- that the **MYIR board file** works, or that a custom platform is needed,
- the real wall-clock cost of one synthesis iteration on 6 cores.

Failing fast there costs a day. Discovering it after T1.3 costs GPU-days of training first.
